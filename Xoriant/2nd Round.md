5. Apart from **managing CI/CD pipelines**, what other **responsibilities** did you have in your **first project**?

6. Were you working **only on DevOps**, or were you also involved in **on-call support**?

7. Was your first assignment at TCS a **single project** for two years, or did you work on **multiple projects**?

8. What kind of **application** was your first project?

9. Can you explain the **architecture** of that application?

10. What kind of **automation** did you implement in that project?

11. After moving to your current company, **how long** have you been working on your **current client project**?

12. What is your **current project** about?

13. Which **part of the project** are you responsible for? What exactly do you **own**?

14. Is your role **purely DevOps**, or do you also have **SRE responsibilities**?

15. You mentioned **on-call support**. What exactly does **on-call support** mean in your project?

16. What are your **day-to-day responsibilities** in your current project?

17. You mentioned you were **monitoring the CI/CD pipelines from a security perspective**. What exactly do you monitor, and what are your responsibilities there?

18. What is your **major contribution** in your current project?

19. You mentioned you received **client appreciation**. What exactly did you do that resulted in that appreciation?

20. What was your **individual contribution**? What exactly did **you implement yourself**?

21. You said you **wrote a Jenkins pipeline end-to-end**. Can you explain exactly what you implemented?

22. You mentioned supporting **on-call activities**. What kind of **production support** did you provide?

23. Can you explain **one major P1/P2 production issue** that you handled? Walk me through the complete story, including the **RCA**, troubleshooting steps, and resolution.

24. What is your contribution with respect to **automation**?

25. Which **programming languages** are you comfortable with?

26. Can you explain **one major automation** that you implemented in your project?

27. Can you explain **how Kubernetes Ingress works**?

28. Suppose a production application is down and a Pod is in **CrashLoopBackOff** state. What would be your **first troubleshooting steps**?

29. What is your contribution with respect to **Terraform**?

30. Suppose the application is **unable to connect to the database after deployment**. How would you troubleshoot it?

31. Suppose your **Terraform state file becomes corrupted**. How would you recover it?

32. **Where do you store** your Terraform state file?

33. How do you **prevent or avoid Terraform state corruption** and similar problems?

34. Have you ever **faced a Terraform state corruption issue** in your project? If yes, explain what happened.

35. Suppose a **container starts and exits within a few seconds** after deployment. How would you debug it, and what commands would you use?

36. Suppose users suddenly cannot access the application because the **SSL certificate has expired**. How would you verify and troubleshoot the issue?

37. Do you have **any questions for me** about the role or project?

38. This role is focused on **DevOps and Python automation**. **How good are you with Python?**

39. Can you **write a Python program** to solve the following problem: **Find the shortest unique prefix for each word in a given array**?
